## Key Question 1 — Which oxygenate / C1 papers has the group published, and what are their product distributions?

### Takeaway
The Guo–Song (DUT / PSU-DUT Joint Center) oxygenate line is dominated by **methanol** (Pd–Cu/SiO2 2015–2019; In2O3/ZrO2 2020; ZnAl2O4 and Cu/ZnAl2O4 spinels 2021–2025) and **higher alcohols over modified iron carbide** (Mn–K–Fe5C2, Mn–Cu–K–Fe5C2 + CuZnAlZr tandem, 2022–2023, ~13.5–15.5% HA selectivity at ~42% CO2 conversion). C1 non-oxygenate work is **Co-based methanation / CO** (Co/ZrO2 ~99.9% CH4; Co/TiO2 phase switch rutile→CH4 vs anatase→CO). I found **no confirmed Guo Xinwen paper on DME, ethanol-selective catalysts, or Rh catalysts**; several "Guo et al." ethanol/HA papers in the literature belong to other Guos (see confusion list).
